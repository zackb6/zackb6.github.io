The fastest growing MMA Organization

The Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) is the world's biggest MMA company. It was founded in 1993 and was originally designed to find out the best fighting style. Early events featured people from different fighting styles, such as boxing, BJJ, and wrestling, competing against each other. The UFC has come a long way from where it started.

Today, the UFC has evolved into a major global sport with a unified set of rules and a range of weight classes. The fighters now are mostly well-rounded, trained in multiple different disciplines. Fights take place in an cage called "The Octagon," (which originally scared a lot of people off) and a winner is determined by knockout, submission, or a judge vote after 3 or 5 rounds.
