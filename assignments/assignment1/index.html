The Ohio State Buckeyes Football Program

The Ohio State football team plays in the Big Ten Conference. The program is one of the most successful in all of college football history. They have multiple national championships, most recently in 2024, and a massive, passionate fanbase, they are a perennial powerhouse. Their home games are played at Ohio Stadium, which is known as The Horseshoe.

Ohio State is also known for their traditions that are loved by students and fans. The marching band, which is known as "The Best Damn Band in the Land," is famous for its Script Ohio formation, where one person is given the opportunity to dot the I in Ohio. Another tradition is the planting of a buckeye tree in Buckeye Grove for each player who earns first-team All-American honors. These traditions contribute to the unique and amazing atmosphere of Ohio State football.
